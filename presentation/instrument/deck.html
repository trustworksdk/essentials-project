1 — Modeling1 — Modellering/a new requirement

“Two instruments must not trade under the same symbol.”“To instrumenter må ikke handle under samme symbol.”

This rule is not about one instrument. It is about all of them.

Denne regel handler ikke om ét instrument. Den handler om dem alle.

Why the aggregate cannot enforce itHvorfor aggregatet ikke kan håndhæve den An aggregate is the consistency boundary: it sees its own stream and nothing else. To check a symbol against every other instrument it would have to load every instrument in one transaction — which is not what a boundary is for, and does not scale. So the decision moves up one level, into the handler, and the handler asks the instrument_details view from its own context. Et aggregate er consistency boundary'en: det ser sin egen stream og intet andet. For at tjekke et symbol mod alle andre instrumenter skulle det loade hvert instrument i én transaktion — hvilket ikke er hvad en boundary er til, og ikke skalerer. Så beslutningen flytter et niveau op, ind i handleren, og handleren spørger instrument_details-viewet fra sit eget kontekst.

The filter keeps INV-RI-2 intact: the same registration retried still succeeds silently. Only a different instrument claiming the symbol is rejected.

filter holder INV-RI-2 intakt: samme registrering gentaget lykkes stadig lydløst. Kun et andet instrument der gør krav på symbolet afvises.

The textbook name for this shape is set-based validation — worth knowing, because every event-sourced system meets it.

Fagudtrykket for denne form er set-based validation — værd at kende, for hvert event-sourced system møder den.

1 — Modeling1 — Modellering/requirement three

“An external risk service must approve it first.”“En ekstern risk-service skal godkende først.”

Why the transaction must end firstHvorfor transaktionen skal slutte først Holding a connection and the aggregate's locks for a network round trip is the obvious cost. But the real reason is that a rollback cannot un-call an external service, and a retried transaction calls it a second time. Commit first, then call. At holde en connection og aggregatets låse under en netværks-rundtur er den åbenlyse omkostning. Men den egentlige grund er at et rollback ikke kan af-kalde en ekstern service, og en genkørt transaktion kalder den en anden gang. Commit først, kald derefter.

EventProcessor is built for exactly this — the docs list its role as external integrations and long operations. Inbox-backed, so the obligation is durable; exclusive via a FencedLock; retried on a policy you set; dead-letters when the service stays down.

EventProcessor er bygget til præcis dette — docs angiver dens rolle som eksterne integrationer og langvarige operationer. Inbox-baseret, så forpligtelsen er durable; eksklusiv via en FencedLock; gentages efter en policy du sætter; dead-letter'er når servicen bliver nede.

GainGevinst

No network call inside a transaction. The obligation survives a restart and is visible in the admin console. The deadline is a delayed command, not a cron job.

Intet netværkskald inde i en transaktion. Forpligtelsen overlever en genstart og er synlig i admin-konsollen. Deadline er en delayed command, ikke et cron-job.

Because two events written a microsecond apart, on two instances with clocks that disagree, can come back in the wrong order — and in an event-sourced system the wrong order is the wrong answer, permanently. So order is an assigned number, and the timestamp is only ever information for a human. 05 — EventProcessor05 — EventProcessor/read models

Project by default. Read the aggregate only with a reason.Projicér som standard. Læs aggregatet kun med en grund.

What a projection isHvad en projektion er A subscriber that reads the event stream and writes an ordinary table shaped for one question. You can have as many as you have questions, they are rebuilt by replaying — that is what onSubscriptionsReset is for — and a rebuild is a button, not a migration. The catch is lag: a read taken immediately after the write that feeds it can still show the previous value. En subscriber der læser event-streamen og skriver en almindelig tabel formet til ét spørgsmål. Man kan have lige så mange som man har spørgsmål, de genopbygges ved replay — det er hvad onSubscriptionsReset er til — og en genopbygning er en knap, ikke en migrering. Prisen er forsinkelse: en læsning lige efter den skrivning der fodrer den kan stadig vise den forrige værdi.

Why the exception is allowed: the bootstrap probe asks whether a price already exists, and an eventually-consistent “absent” would let it seed a second time on top of live data. latestPrice() is the only public accessor on any aggregate in the demo.

Hvorfor undtagelsen er tilladt: bootstrap-proben spørger om en pris allerede findes, og et eventually-consistent “fraværende” ville lade den seede en anden gang oven i levende data. latestPrice() er den eneste offentlige accessor på noget aggregate i demoen.

06 — snapshots06 — snapshots

Long streams cost more to load. One annotation fixes it.Lange streams koster mere at loade. Én annotation løser det.

In plain termsKort sagt Loading an aggregate means replaying its events, so a stream with 50 000 events costs 50 000 events' worth of work. A snapshot is a saved copy of the aggregate at event n, so loading becomes “read the snapshot, then replay only what came after it”. At loade et aggregate betyder at replaye dets events, så en stream med 50.000 events koster 50.000 events' arbejde. Et snapshot er en gemt kopi af aggregatet ved event n, så load bliver “læs snapshottet, replay derefter kun hvad der kom efter”.

Instrument declares no policy — registered once, rarely renamed. A short stream is cheaper to replay than a snapshot is to maintain.

Instrument deklarerer ingen policy — registreres én gang, omdøbes sjældent. En kort stream er billigere at replaye end et snapshot er at vedligeholde.

GainGevinst

Load cost stops growing with history. ASYNC_DURABLE keeps the snapshot off the write path.

Load-omkostningen holder op med at vokse med historikken. ASYNC_DURABLE holder snapshottet ude af write-stien.

CostOmkostning

The snapshot lands slightly behind, and its serialized shape becomes a second format you must keep readable.

Snapshottet lander lidt bagefter, og dets serialiserede form bliver et andet format du skal holde læsbart.

08 — DurableQueues + FencedLock08 — DurableQueues + FencedLock

Queues and locks in the database you already runKøer og låse i den database du allerede driver

Why a fence token, not just a lockHvorfor et fence token, ikke bare en lås A holder can stall — a long GC pause, a paused VM — lose the lock to someone else, then wake up still believing it holds it. A plain lock cannot tell the difference. The token is a number that only ever increases, so whatever the holder writes to can check it and reject the stale one. En holder kan gå i stå — en lang GC-pause, en pauset VM — miste låsen til en anden og så vågne i troen om at den stadig holder den. En almindelig lås kan ikke se forskellen. Tokenet er et tal der kun stiger, så det den skriver til kan tjekke det og afvise det forældede.

GainGevinst

No broker, no ZooKeeper, no Redis. The queue commits in the same transaction as your data, and a stale token gets rejected.

Ingen broker, ingen ZooKeeper, ingen Redis. Køen committer i samme transaktion som dine data, og et forældet token afvises.

CostOmkostning

A throughput ceiling, and load on the database. Intra-service only — instances of one service, one database. Not cross-service.

Et throughput-loft, og belastning på databasen. Kun intra-service — instanser af én service, én database. Ikke på tværs af services.

The dual writeDual write Two systems, two writes, no shared transaction. Commit the order and the process dies before the Kafka send: the order exists and nobody downstream knows. Send first and it dies before the commit: everyone downstream believes in an order that does not exist. The outbox makes it one write — the message goes into your database, in the same transaction, and a relay ships it afterwards. To systemer, to skrivninger, ingen fælles transaktion. Commit ordren og processen dør før Kafka-afsendelsen: ordren findes, og ingen downstream ved det. Send først, og den dør før commit: alle downstream tror på en ordre der ikke findes. Outboxen gør det til én skrivning — beskeden lander i din database, i samme transaktion, og et relay sender den bagefter.

Inbox is the mirror image: an incoming message is stored and de-duplicated before it is handled.

Inbox er spejlbilledet: en indkommende besked gemmes og de-duplikeres før den håndteres.

GainGevinst

A message is never published for a transaction that rolled back, and never lost for one that committed.

En besked publiceres aldrig for en transaktion der rullede tilbage, og mistes aldrig for en der committede.

CostOmkostning

At-least-once, not exactly-once. Every downstream consumer must be idempotent — that is now their design constraint too.

At-least-once, ikke exactly-once. Hver downstream-consumer skal være idempotent — det er nu også deres designbegrænsning.

Where it does not fitHvor det ikke passer

  • Intra-service only. Locks, queues, inbox and outbox coordinate instances of one service against a shared database. They are not cross-service infrastructure.
  • Kun intra-service. Låse, køer, inbox og outbox koordinerer instanser af én service mod en delt database. De er ikke infrastruktur på tværs af services.
  • An event's persisted type is its class name. This demo's own README tells you to wipe the database, because the slice refactor renamed every event class. Renames cost an upcaster or a migration.
  • Et events persisterede type er dets klassenavn. Denne demos egen README beder dig slette databasen, fordi slice-refaktoreringen omdøbte hver event-klasse. Omdøbninger koster en upcaster eller en migrering.
  • Integrations are provided scope. Consumers declare their own third-party versions. Deliberate, and occasionally annoying.
  • Integrationer er provided scope. Consumers deklarerer selv deres third-party-versioner. Bevidst, og indimellem irriterende.
  • Table and collection names are concatenated into queries. Validate them or hard-code them — there is a checker for exactly this.
  • Tabel- og collection-navne konkateneres ind i queries. Validér dem eller hardcode dem — der findes en checker til præcis dette.
  • Two Jackson majors, one wire format. Byte-identical JSON across both, which is what keeps old data readable — and means a serialization change must be run on both build profiles.
  • To Jackson-majorversioner, ét wire-format. Byte-identisk JSON på tværs af begge, hvilket holder gamle data læsbare — og betyder at en serialiseringsændring skal køres på begge build-profiler.
  • Aggregates are not a write-rate solution. You just watched the benchmark.
  • Aggregates er ikke en løsning på skrivehastighed. Du har lige set benchmarket.

None of these are surprises in production. They are surprises in week two, which is cheaper.

Ingen af disse er overraskelser i produktion. De er overraskelser i uge to, hvilket er billigere.

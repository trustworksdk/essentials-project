Warehouse — shipping (to-do list, human trigger)

OrderShip toMethodState
nothing to pack

Rows appear when OrderPlaced is projected, carry the order through packing and dispatch, and leave on OrderShipped or OrderCancelled. Packing a card order asks the gateway for the money, so the row waits in AWAITING_PAYMENT until FundsCaptured arrives on the webhook — a hold is a promise, and the parcel waits for the money rather than the promise. A refusal, before or after packing, leaves the row visible with no button: this read model is the guard, because a decider cannot see another context's stream.

Captures awaiting outcome — payment (the unknowns)

OrderIdempotency keyAmountWaiting
nothing outstanding

A row here is money in an unknown state: we asked the gateway to charge the card and have no answer yet. Rows normally vanish within a second or two. One that stays is what a lost callback looks like — the reconciler asks the gateway directly once it has waited long enough, because a timeout is not a failure, it is an unknown, and the only way to resolve an unknown is to ask.
